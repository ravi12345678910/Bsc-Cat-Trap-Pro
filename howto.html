Simple to Learn

How to play in 5 steps

01

Start the Game

Click the "Start Game" button to begin. The cat will start moving randomly around the game area.

02

Catch the Cat

Click on the cat as quickly as possible. Each successful click increases your score by 1 point.

💡 Tip: Build combos by clicking rapidly without missing!
03

Watch the Timer

You have 30 seconds per level. The timer turns red when time is running low!

04

Earn Stars

Reach the score targets to earn stars. Get 3 stars to advance to the next level!

05

Level Up

Each new level makes the cat move faster! Can you reach level 10?

Combo System

Click rapidly without missing to build a combo multiplier!

1 = 1 point
2 = 2 points
5 = 5x multiplier!
10 = 10x MAX!
Normal click: +1 point
5x Combo: +5 points
10x Combo: +10 points

The higher your combo, the more points you earn per click! Max combo is 10x!
